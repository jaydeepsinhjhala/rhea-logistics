Our Services

Comprehensive logistic solutions tailored to your business needs.

Transportation

Rhea Logistics is equipped with Containerized Trucks and Body Trucks. We make our repertoire of services available to clients across the country through our network of offices crisscrossing major cities and ports.

Freight Forwarding

We provide forwarding and shipping services to clients. With infrastructure catering to all needs of shippers, importers, and exporters, we offer best freight rates through contracts with various liners. We link Kandla, Mundra, and Pipavav to international destinations.

Warehousing

We have warehouse space available in and around Kandla & Mundra and its hinterland. Equipped with the latest communication and automation facilities, our warehousing is an integral link in the logistics chain.

Other Customized Services

  • Custom Brokerage CHA, Project Cargo Handling, Packing and Palletization
  • Special - ODC (Over Dimensional Cargo)
  • Fumigation Phyto, ADC and PHO services
  • Pre-Shipment Advice and Documentation, Drawback Claims, Domestic Railway Clearance
  • Central Excise Refund, Claim & Bond, Proof of Exports
  • DEPB / DFIA / DFRC / Verification and DEEC Audit
  • Certificate of Origin, D.G.F.T Related Services
  • GSP Certificate, Documents legalization from consulates
